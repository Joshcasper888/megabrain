## 1. What Apex does
Apex builds, writes, runs and optimizes Facebook and Instagram ads for the Client, and sends every lead to the Client's phone or email as soon as it comes in. Apex does not launch or change any ad without the Client's approval of the first campaign. Apex sends a short weekly update: leads, cost per lead, and what is being changed.

## 2. Ad spend
The Client pays Meta directly with the Client's own card, at **$____ per day** (suggested $20–$50). The Client can pause or change the budget at any time. Apex never pays for or holds the Client's ad spend. Ad spend is separate from Apex's fee.

## 3. Apex's fee (Client picks one)

☐ **Option A: Pay per lead, $30 per lead.**
A "lead" is a form or message from a real person in the service area with a working name and phone number, asking about the Client's services. Duplicates, spam, wrong numbers and people outside the service area do not count. The Client can flag a bad lead within 7 days and it is removed from the bill. Apex invoices at the end of each month with a list of every lead billed.

☐ **Option B: Percentage, 15% of jobs from the ads.**
15% of the amount the Client collects on jobs that came from an Apex ad lead. At the end of each month the Client sends a short list of those jobs (customer name and job total). Nothing else about the Client's business is shared. Apex invoices after the Client is paid for the job.

☐ **Option C: Flat monthly fee, $____ per month.**
A set fee billed at the start of each month, no matter how many leads come in. The Client never has to share revenue, job values, books or any other business numbers with Apex.

**Payment:** Invoices are due within 15 days.

## 4. Term and cancelling
Month to month, with no long-term contract. Either side can end it with written notice (text or email is fine). Notice takes effect at the end of the current month. Under Options A and B, the Client pays only for leads or jobs from before the end date.

## 5. Ownership
The Client owns the ad account, Facebook Page, pixel, ads and all leads. Apex has partner access only, and the Client can remove it at any time.

## 6. No guarantees
Apex will do its best work, but cannot guarantee a set number of leads or jobs. Results depend on the market, budget, offer and how fast leads are called back.
