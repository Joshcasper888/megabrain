## 1. What Apex does
Apex builds, writes, runs and optimizes Facebook and Instagram ads for the Client, and sends every lead to the Client's phone or email as soon as it comes in. Apex does not launch or change any ad without the Client's approval of the first campaign.

## 2. Ad spend
The Client pays Meta directly with the Client's own card, at **$____ per day** (suggested $20–$50). The Client can pause or change the budget at any time. Apex never pays for or holds the Client's ad spend.

## 3. Free 30-day trial
Apex charges **no fee for the first 30 days**. At the end of the trial the Client can stop with no cost and keeps every lead.

## 4. After the trial (only if the Client continues)
- **Fee:** 15% of the collected revenue from jobs that came from an Apex-run ad or lead form. No jobs, no fee.
- **What counts:** a job counts if the customer first came in through an Apex ad or lead form while this agreement is active.
- **Reporting:** by the 5th of each month, the Client sends a list of closed jobs from ad leads, with job value. Apex keeps its own log of every lead delivered, for comparison.
- **Invoicing:** Apex invoices monthly, and payment is due within 15 days.
- **Tail:** jobs from leads delivered during the agreement that close within 90 days after it ends still count.

## 5. Term and cancelling
Month to month. Either side can end it with written notice (text or email is fine). During the trial, notice takes effect immediately; after the trial, 30 days' notice.

## 6. Ownership
The Client owns the ad account, Facebook Page, pixel, ads and all leads. Apex has partner access only, and the Client can remove it at any time.

## 7. No guarantees
Apex will do its best work, but cannot guarantee a set number of leads or jobs. Results depend on the market, budget, offer and how fast leads are called back.
