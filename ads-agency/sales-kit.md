## 2. Objection handling

| They say | You say |
|---|---|
| "We tried Facebook ads, they don't work." | "Most contractors who say that ran boosted posts or a generic 'free estimate' ad. What did yours look like?" Then listen and point to the fix. |
| "I get enough work from referrals." | "Great, that means you do good work. Ads just let you pick the jobs you want and fill slow weeks. Are you booked solid all winter too?" |
| "Leads from ads are junk." | "The form asks if they own the home, what service they need, and their zip code before they submit. You only get homeowners in your area." |
| "I already have a marketing guy." | "Totally fine. Is he paid whether or not you get jobs? I'm only paid when you do. Happy to run one test next to him." |
| "What's the catch with 15%?" | "No catch. It keeps me honest. If I don't bring jobs, I don't eat. You do need to tell me which leads closed each month; that's it." |
| "How much do I spend on ads?" | "You set it. Most start at $30–$50 a day for roofing, $20–$30 for landscaping, paid straight to Meta on your own card. You can pause any time." |
| "Send me some info." | "Sure. What's the best email? And so it's actually useful, what kind of job do you most want more of?" (Then send the free plan, not a brochure.) |

## 4. Revenue-share terms to agree on before starting

*(An outline to discuss with the client, not legal advice. Have a short written
agreement reviewed before your first signed client.)*

1. **What counts:** a job counts if the customer came in through an Apex-run ad or lead
   form, and the lead came in while the agreement was active.
2. **Reporting:** the client sends a list of closed jobs from ad leads, with job value,
   by the 5th of each month. Apex keeps its own list of every lead delivered, to compare.
3. **Fee:** 15% of the collected job revenue, invoiced monthly, due in 15 days.
4. **Ad spend:** paid by the client directly to Meta. Set a minimum monthly spend (for
   example, $900) so there's enough data to optimize.
5. **Term:** month to month, 30 days' notice.
6. **Tail:** jobs from leads delivered during the agreement that close within 90 days
   after it ends still count. Roofing jobs often take weeks to close.
7. **Ownership:** the client owns the ad account, Page, pixel and leads. Apex has
   partner access only.

**Watch out:** a $15k roof pays you $2,250, which is great, but only if they report it.
Choose clients you trust, and ask to see their CRM or job board on the monthly call.
