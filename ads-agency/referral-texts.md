# Referral Texts (send after a client's first good month)

*Owners know other owners. One referral from a happy client is worth 100 cold calls. Offer a free month of your fee for every referral that signs.*

## 1. After a good first month
> "Hey [Name], your ads brought in [#] leads last month. Glad it's working! Quick question: do you know any other contractors (landscaping, tree, roofing, masonry) who could use more calls? If anyone you send signs up, your next month is on me."

## 2. Short version
> "[Name], if you know anyone in the trades who wants more leads, send them my way. Your next month's free if they sign up. Thanks!"

## 3. When they say "yes, I know someone"
> "Awesome, thank you! Just send me their name and number, or give them mine: [your number]. I'll take good care of them."

## 4. Text to the person they referred
> "Hi [Name], this is Josh with ApexLeads. [Client] said you might want more jobs coming in from Facebook. I run their ads and they got [#] leads last month. Want me to take a look at your page and show you what I'd do? No cost to look."

## 5. Thank-you when a referral signs
> "[Name], [Referral] just signed on. Thank you! Your next month's fee is covered. Really appreciate it."

## When to ask
- After the first month with good results.
- Right after they tell you something good ("we booked 3 jobs off those!").
- Every 3 months after that.
