**How to use it:**
- **Ask for the owner by name** ("Hey, is Mike around?"), then give the hook.
- ⭐ = you have an **ad pack** in `spec-ads/` to text right after the call.
- Close every call with: *"What's the best number to reach you directly?"* and
  *"Can I text you a quick ad I made for you?"*
- Tell Claude the outcome (reached / voicemail / interested / no) so the CRM stays current.

**Core hook:** *"I saw your Facebook ad. [Their problem]. I only run ads for
[roofers/landscapers], and I don't charge anything unless the ads bring you jobs. Can I
text you a fixed version?"*
