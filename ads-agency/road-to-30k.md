# ApexLeads: Road to $30k/Month

## The math
- At $900/month: **34 clients**. At $1,500/month: **20 clients**.
- Plan for about 20% of clients leaving each year, so the real target is about **25 clients at $1,200+**.
- Today: 0 clients, 5 warm leads, about 1,650 companies in the pipeline.

**Honest timeline:** working part-time, $30k/month is roughly **12–18 months away**, and that's if you hit every milestone below. Going full-time, or adding a caller, cuts that a lot. Nobody gets there without client #1, so that's the whole focus right now.

## Phase 1: Get client #1 (now → Nov 15)
**What's going wrong:** people say "interested" and then go quiet. The fix is to **close on the call**, not after it.

1. **Call every hot lead this week** (Everest207, Premium Tree, Tree Busters, EML, Nature Bound). Don't text; call.
2. **New close process:** "Interested" → "Let's do a 15-minute Zoom tomorrow and I'll show you the ads I made for you" → on that Zoom, show the spec ads, pick a price option, send the agreement, and collect the first payment while you're still talking.
3. **Take away their risk** (this is not a free trial): *"If you don't get at least 10 leads in your first 30 days, your second month is free."* You still get paid, and they can't lose.
4. **Get paid easily:** set up Square or Stripe invoices so they can pay by card during the call.
5. **Keep dialing:** 15–20 calls a day from the call sheets (★22, ★23). Phone calls get responses; your cold emails got zero.

## Phase 2: First 3 clients and proof (Nov → Dec)
6. **Over-deliver for client #1:** respond to leads within 5 minutes, send a weekly report, and call them weekly.
7. **Winter pitch** (landscaping slows down): tree companies get **storm damage and removal** ads; landscapers get **snow removal** now and **spring patio and cleanup pre-booking** from January to March.
8. **Case study after 30 days:** leads, cost per lead and jobs booked. That becomes your best sales tool.
9. **Referral offer:** a free month for every client they refer who signs.

## Phase 3: Spring rush, 8–15 clients (Jan → Jun)
10. **February–April is when landscapers buy.** Have 100+ warm contacts lined up by February.
11. **Raise prices once you have proof:** new clients at $1,200, early clients stay at what they signed.
12. **Upsell:** an instant text-back to every lead for +$150/month, and a review-request system for +$100/month.
13. **Run ApexLeads' own ads** once Facebook restores your account, using the case study.

## Phase 4: Stop doing everything yourself, 15–25+ clients (Jul → Dec 2027)
14. **Hire a caller** (part-time, paid per booked call). You close and manage the ads.
15. **Same setup for every client:** templates and a 2-hour onboarding. Claude builds the ads, reports and lead lists.
16. **In-person reach:** landscape supply yards, the Massachusetts Nursery & Landscape Association, and trade shows (New England Grows is in winter).

## Rules that protect the business
- **Your Facebook profile is the business.** Clients add you as a partner on *their* ad accounts. Manage client ads yourself in Ads Manager. Don't let any automation make changes to them, because that is what got your own account disabled.
- **Never create extra Facebook profiles or ad accounts.**
- **Promise the work, not the result.** Don't guarantee a number of jobs.
- **Track everything in the pipeline,** and tell Claude after every call session.
