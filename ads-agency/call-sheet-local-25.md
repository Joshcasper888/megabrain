**Why I couldn't check their ads:** Facebook blocks the ad search while your ad account is disabled. So before each call, take 20 seconds on your phone:
1. Go to **facebook.com/ads/library**, set the country to US, and type the company name.
2. **Running a broken ad** (blank headline, "fb.com", copied ads, old season) → use **Opener A**.
