# Weekly Client Report (template)

*Send every Monday by text or email. It should take the client 30 seconds to read. Fill in the blanks from Ads Manager, or ask Claude to pull the numbers for you.*

---

**Subject / first line:** [Company] ads: week of [date]

Hi [Owner],

Here's how your ads did last week:

- **Leads:** [#] new leads ([#] the week before)
- **Cost per lead:** $[##]
- **Ad spend:** $[###] (paid directly to Facebook on your card)
- **Best ad:** "[ad name / headline]" brought in [#] leads

**What I changed this week:**
- [e.g., Turned off the stump grinding ad: it cost $60 per lead]
- [e.g., Moved that budget to the fall cleanup ad, now at $22 per lead]
- [e.g., Added a new patio ad for spring pre-booking]

**What I need from you (if anything):**
- [e.g., 3–5 new job photos, especially patios]
- [e.g., Did the leads from Tuesday turn into estimates? Just reply "yes" or "no"]

**Quick tip:** Call every lead within 5 minutes if you can. It roughly doubles how many turn into jobs.

Talk soon,
Josh, ApexLeads

---

## Monthly version (first Monday of each month)
Add these lines to the weekly report:
- **Month total:** [#] leads at $[##] each
- **Jobs booked from ads (if they'll share):** [#]
- **Plan for next month:** [1–2 sentences: new offer, season change, budget change]

## Where to find the numbers
- Ads Manager → Campaigns → set the date range to "Last 7 days".
- Columns: Results (leads), Cost per result, Amount spent.
- Or ask Claude: "pull last week's numbers for [client]".
