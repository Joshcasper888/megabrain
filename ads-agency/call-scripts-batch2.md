## The core script (use for everyone)

**1. Ask for the owner by first name**
> "Hi, is **{Name}** around?"

If someone asks what it's about: *"It's about how {Company} shows up on Facebook in
{City}. Two minutes."*

**2. Opener**
> "{Name}, it's Josh with ApexRoofingLeads. *(If status is 'contacted':)* I emailed you
> this morning. Quick one: are you trying to book more {roof / landscape} jobs right
> now, or are you slammed?"

**3. Hook (the company-specific line from the table below)**
> "{Hook}. But homeowners scrolling Facebook in {City} don't know that. They're seeing
> other companies' ads."

**4. Offer**
> "All I do is Facebook ads for {roofers / landscapers}. You pay Facebook directly for
> the ads, and I don't take a fee for the first 30 days. If it brings you jobs, we keep
> going. If not, you walk away and keep every lead."

**5. Close**
> "Let me put together a free plan for {Company}. Can I walk you through it for
> 15 minutes, Thursday or Friday?"

*Get the day, time and their email before hanging up.*

**Voicemail**
> "Hey {Name}, Josh with ApexRoofingLeads. {Hook, one sentence}. I've got a quick idea
> to get more {City} homeowners calling you. No fee for 30 days. Call me back at ___."
