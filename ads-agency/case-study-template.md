# Case Study Template (fill in after a client's first good month)

*This is the most valuable sales tool you'll have. One real result makes cold calls easier. Get the client's OK before using their name. If they say no, use "a landscaper in southern Maine".*

---

## Short version (for calls and texts)

> "Last month I ran ads for [a landscaper in Wells, Maine / Company Name]. They got **[#] leads at $[##] each** and booked **[#] jobs** worth about **$[#,###]**. Their ads had been running with no offer before that. I can do the same for you."

## Full version (for a Google Doc, website or Facebook post)

**Company:** [Name / "A family-owned landscaper in ___"]
**Location:** [Town, State]
**Services:** [e.g., fall cleanups, patios, tree removal]

**Before:**
- [e.g., Same ad copied 6 times, headline was just the company name]
- [e.g., About 0–2 calls a month from Facebook]

**What we did:**
- [e.g., 3 new ads: fall cleanup, spring patio pre-booking, reviews]
- [e.g., Lead form so homeowners send their info without leaving Facebook]
- [e.g., Instant text to the owner for every lead, weekly testing]

**Results (first 30 days):**
- **[#] leads** at **$[##] per lead**
- **[#] jobs booked**, about **$[#,###]** in work
- Ad spend: $[###]

**Owner quote:**
> "[Ask: 'In one sentence, what's been different since we started?']"

**Screenshots to save:**
- Ads Manager results (leads + cost per lead, dates visible)
- 2–3 lead notifications (hide the homeowner's name and phone)
- The before and after ad

---

## How to ask for the testimonial (text)
> "Hey [Name], glad the ads have been working. Could I share your results (leads and cost, no private numbers) with other contractors? And would you give me one sentence on how it's been going? Totally fine if not."
