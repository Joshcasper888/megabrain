# ApexLeads: Ads to Get Clients Coming to You

*Run these only after you have 1 real client result, so the ads can use real numbers. Budget: start at $10–15/day. Nothing launches without your OK.*

## Who to target
- **Location:** Massachusetts, Rhode Island, Connecticut, New Hampshire, Maine and Vermont.
- **Audience:** business owners with interests in landscaping, lawn care, arborist/tree care, hardscaping, or "small business owners".
- **Ages:** 25–60.
- **Placements:** Facebook Feed plus Instagram Feed.
- **Goal:** leads (instant form). Questions on the form: business name, what service you want more of, best phone number.
