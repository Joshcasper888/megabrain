## Facebook ad account appeal

**Where:** facebook.com/accountquality → select the disabled ad account (ending 4252) → **Request review**. If Facebook asks for ID, complete identity verification; that is normal and often what gets the account back.

**What to write (paste and edit if needed):**

> Hello,
>
> My ad account (ID 1106381341794252) was disabled for unusual activity. I'm a small business owner in Blackstone, Massachusetts. I run ApexLeads, and I'm advertising my own service to local landscaping and tree companies.
>
> I set up my campaign with help from an AI assistant that I connected to my own account. I've since stopped all automated changes to the account and I'll manage it myself in Ads Manager going forward. I'm the only person who uses this account. I'm happy to verify my identity.
>
> The ads promote my real business and use my own Facebook page (ApexLeads). I also had a small unpaid balance of $2.13 from a declined card, which I'll pay as soon as the account allows it.
>
> Thank you for reviewing my account.
>
> Joshua Casper-Phipps

**Important:**
- Submit **one** appeal and wait. Repeated appeals don't help.
- **Don't** create a new ad account or page while the appeal is open. Facebook can link it to the disabled one and disable that too.
- Reviews can take from a day to a few weeks. Check your email and Account Quality.
