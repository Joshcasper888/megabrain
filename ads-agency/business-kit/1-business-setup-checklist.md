# ApexLeads: Business Setup Checklist

Do these in order. Most cost little or nothing. Check prices on the official sites before paying, because fees change.

## Week 1 (free or close to free)
- [x] **Business phone number:** (508) 492-9796 (now on your cards, website and service sheet). Add it to your Facebook page too.
- [ ] **Business email:** keep apexroofingleads1@gmail.com for now. Once you buy a domain, switch to an address like josh@[yourdomain], since "roofing" in the current address doesn't match landscaping.
- [ ] **Facebook page:** finish the About section, add the phone number and service area, set the profile picture to the logo and the cover photo to the service sheet.
- [ ] **Facebook ad account appeal:** see `3-email-signature-and-appeal.md`.

## Before your first client pays you
- [ ] **Business name registration (DBA):** if you stay a sole proprietor, file a business certificate ("doing business as ApexLeads") with the **Blackstone Town Clerk**. The fee is small; call the clerk to confirm it.
- [ ] **EIN:** free from the IRS at irs.gov (search "apply for EIN online"). Takes about 10 minutes. Never pay a website for this.
- [ ] **Business bank account:** open a free business checking account using the DBA or LLC paperwork and EIN. Keep all agency money in it, in and out.
- [ ] **Way to get paid:** Venmo Business, Zelle through the business bank, or Stripe / Square invoices (card fees are about 3%).

## When you have 2–3 paying clients
- [ ] **LLC:** file a Certificate of Organization with the Massachusetts Secretary of the Commonwealth (sec.state.ma.us). The state fee is about $500 to file and about $500 each year for the annual report. An LLC protects your personal assets if a client ever has a dispute with you.
- [ ] **Domain and website:** buy a domain with ApexLeads in the name (about $12–20/year) and point it at the website.
- [ ] **Simple bookkeeping:** a spreadsheet is fine to start: date, client, amount in, ad tools out.

## Taxes (important)
- [ ] Set aside **25–30% of every payment** in a separate savings account for taxes. No one withholds taxes from agency income.
- [ ] Self-employed people usually pay **quarterly estimated taxes** (federal: IRS Form 1040-ES; Massachusetts: Form 1-ES). A local accountant can set this up in one visit.
- [ ] Keep receipts for business costs: phone, software, printing business cards, mileage to meet clients.

*This is a general checklist, not legal or tax advice. A one-time meeting with a local accountant is worth it once money starts coming in.*
