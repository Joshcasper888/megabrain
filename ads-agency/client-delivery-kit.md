# ApexRoofingLeads Client Delivery Kit

What to do from the moment a client says yes, so the first leads arrive within days.

---

## 1. Onboarding: the day a client says yes

**Send the client** [`client-onboarding.pdf`](client-onboarding.pdf) (client-facing, one page).
It covers ad account access, Page access, and the photos and info we need.

**Text to send with it:**
> "Awesome, [Name]. Two things to get started: (1) the trial agreement, sign and send
> it back, and (2) this 10-minute setup sheet so I can get into your ad account. Once
> I'm in, I'll have your ads built for you to approve within 1–2 days."

**Josh, same day:**
- [ ] Fill in and send `trial-agreement.pdf`. Don't build anything until it's signed.
- [ ] Send `client-onboarding.pdf`, and put your Google Voice number in the blank at the bottom.
- [ ] Mark the lead **"client"** in the CRM, with the start date in the notes.
- [ ] Once they say "done", tell Claude: "[Client] gave access". Claude will:
  - confirm their ad account and Page show up in the Meta Ads connection
  - check that their Page has accepted Meta's lead-ad terms. If not, the client
    clicks "accept" at facebook.com/legal/leadgen/tos (logged in as a Page admin).
  - build the campaign below **paused**, and send you a preview to show the client
- [ ] Client approves the preview, and you tell Claude to go live.
- [ ] Turn on instant lead alerts on the owner's phone (Meta Business Suite app, then
      Notifications, then Leads).
- [ ] Put a reminder on your calendar for day 3 (first check-in) and day 30 (trial ends: keep going?).

**If they get stuck on access,** do it together on a 10-minute video call and have
them share their screen. Menus in Meta move around, so walking them through it live
beats any written steps.

## 2. Campaign templates (Claude builds these in the client's account)

### Roofing: lead form campaign
- **Objective:** Leads → Instant form, **"Higher intent"** form type (adds a review
  step, so fewer junk leads)
- **Budget:** $30–$50/day at the campaign level
- **Location:** client's service radius (zip codes or radius around their shop)
- **Audience:** broad, age 30–65 (homeowners), with Advantage+ audience on
- **Placements:** Advantage+ (automatic)
- **Ads:** 3 to start, each with a different angle (not copies)

**Lead form questions:**
1. Do you own the home? (Yes / No; route "No" to a thank-you screen)
2. What do you need? (Full replacement / Repair or leak / Storm damage / Inspection)
3. Roughly how old is your roof? (Under 10 / 10–20 / 20+ / Not sure)
4. Zip code
5. Name, phone, email (prefilled)

**Ad copy angles:**
- **Inspection offer:** "Free roof inspection + photo report. See exactly what's up
  there before winter. {Town} homeowners only."
- **Financing:** "New roof from $X/month. Get a real number for your home in 60 seconds."
- **Storm/insurance:** "Had wind or hail this year? Damage you can't see from the ground
  can mean a covered roof. Free inspection, no obligation."
  *(Only if the client actually does insurance work, and keep claims factual.)*

### Landscaping: lead form campaign
- Same structure, with a budget of $20–$30/day
- **Form questions:** own the home? / service (Design & install / Hardscape or patio /
  Fall cleanup / Tree work / Ongoing maintenance) / project timing (ASAP / 1–3 months /
  Spring) / zip / contact

**Ad copy angles by season:**
- **Fall (now):** "Leaves, beds, final mow. Get your yard winter-ready. Book fall
  cleanup before the schedule fills."
- **Fall → spring pre-book:** "Want a new patio by summer? Designs done over winter,
  first on the install schedule in spring. Free design consult."
- **Winter (northern clients):** snow and ice contracts for homes and businesses.

**Creative:** the client's own before/after photos, with a text overlay of 5 words or
fewer. Short phone video walkthroughs of finished jobs usually beat polished graphics.

## 3. Optimization routine

| When | What Claude checks and changes |
|---|---|
| Day 3 | Leads coming in? If cost per lead is 2× target and there are no leads, change the offer, not the audience. |
| Weekly | Pause the worst ad, add one new angle. Check that lead quality matches what the client says on the phone. |
| Monthly | Report + revenue-share invoice. Ask for closed-job list. Shift budget to what books jobs, not just what gets leads. |

**Rough targets to judge against** (these vary by market, so use the client's own
first-month numbers once you have them): cost per lead of roughly $30–$80 for roofing
and $20–$50 for landscaping.
