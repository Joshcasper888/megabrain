# Getting Started With ApexLeads

*Takes about 10 minutes. You keep full ownership of everything, and you can remove our access any time.*

## Step 1: Give us access to your ad account
On a computer, go to **facebook.com/adsmanager**, open the menu, and choose **Ad account settings**.

1. Find **Ad account roles** (sometimes shown as **Ad account access**) and choose **Add people**.
2. Search for **Joshua Casper-Phipps** and set the role to **Advertiser**, or **Admin** if Advertiser isn't offered.
3. Click **Confirm**.

*If your ad account is inside Meta Business Suite, go to **Settings → Accounts → Ad accounts**, choose your account, then **Assign partners** or **Add people**, and use the same name.*

**Don't have an ad account yet?** Open **facebook.com/adsmanager** once while logged in and one is created for you. Add your card under **Billing & payments**. You pay Meta directly; we never touch your card.

## Step 2: Give us access to your Facebook Page
On your business Page, go to **Settings → Page access** (or **Page setup → Page access**).

1. Choose **Add new** under people with task access.
2. Search for **Joshua Casper-Phipps** and turn on **Ads**, **Messages** and **Insights**. We don't need full control.

## Step 3: Send us these (text or email is fine)
- [ ] **10–20 photos or short videos** of real jobs. Before-and-after works best, and phone photos are fine.
- [ ] **Service area:** towns or zip codes, and how far you'll drive.
- [ ] **The jobs you want more of**, for example full replacements over repairs, or hardscape over mowing.
- [ ] **An offer you're comfortable with**, for example a free inspection with a photo report, or $X off.
- [ ] **Who gets the leads:** name, cell number and email. Leads come in instantly.
- [ ] **Daily ad budget** (paid to Meta): most start at **$30–50/day for roofing** and **$20–30/day for landscaping**.

## What happens next
- **Within 1–2 days:** we build your campaign, paused, and send you a preview link.
- **You approve it, and it goes live.** Nothing runs until you say yes.
- **Leads go straight to your phone.** Call each one within 5 minutes if you can; fast callbacks book far more jobs.
- **Every week:** a short text update on leads, cost per lead, and what we're changing.
