# ApexRoofingLeads Facebook Page: 15-Minute Setup

**Why this comes first:** every Meta ad runs from a Page, and your login manages none.
Prospects will also look you up after a call, and an empty or missing Page costs you
the deal. Copy and paste everything below.

## 1. Create it
facebook.com/pages/create, logged in as Joshua Casper-Phipps.

| Field | Enter |
|---|---|
| Page name | **ApexRoofingLeads** |
| Category | Advertising/Marketing (add "Marketing Agency" if offered) |
| Bio (max 101 characters) | `Facebook & Instagram ads for roofers and landscapers. No jobs, no fee.` |
| Email | apexroofingleads1@gmail.com |
| Website | leave blank until you have one |
| Hours | Always open, or your real call hours |
| Action button | **Send message** (or **Book now** if you use a calendar link) |

**Profile picture:** a simple logo. A bold "A" or a roofline mark, in one color, works.
You can make one free in Canva in 5 minutes.
**Cover photo:** a crew on a finished roof or a clean landscaped yard, with the text
"More booked jobs. You only pay when the ads work."

## 3. First 3 posts (so the Page isn't empty when prospects check it)

**Post 1: intro**
> We started ApexRoofingLeads with one rule: if our ads don't bring you jobs, you
> don't pay us. We only work with roofers and landscapers, so every ad we write is
> built for homeowners who need exactly what you do. Want a free review of your
> Facebook ads? Send us a message.

**Post 2: a useful tip (shows expertise)**
> 3 things we see in almost every contractor's Facebook ads:
> 1. Same ad, copied 3 times. That isn't testing; Meta just splits your budget.
> 2. "Free estimate" as the whole offer. Every competitor says it. Give a reason to call *now*.
> 3. Slow callbacks. A lead called within 5 minutes is far more likely to book than
>    one called the next day.
> Fix #3 first. It's free.

**Post 3: seasonal**
> Roofers: fall is your window before winter. Homeowners want roofs done before the
> first snow, so ads running now book jobs this month.
> Landscapers: the companies with a full spring calendar start booking in the fall.
> Fall cleanup ads now, spring hardscape pre-booking next.

## 4. Connect it to your ad account
1. Go to Ads Manager (account **1106381341794252**), then **Billing & payments**, and
   **add a card**. Nothing spends until a campaign is switched on.
2. Tell Claude "Page is created". Claude will check it appears for the ad account and
   build your campaign **paused**, with a preview link for you to approve before
   anything goes live.
3. Optional: link an Instagram account in Page settings → Linked accounts, so ads can
   run on Instagram under your own name.
