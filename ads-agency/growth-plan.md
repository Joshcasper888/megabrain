# ApexLeads Growth Plan: Getting to $100k/year

**The math:** $100k a year is about $8,300 a month.
- At $600/month per client → **about 14 clients**
- At $1,000/month per client → **about 9 clients**

You don't need hundreds of clients. You need 10–15 who stay.

## Phase 1: First 1–3 clients (now to about 60 days)
1. Close the warm ones: Everest207 (call booked), Premium Tree, Tree Busters, EML and Nature Bound.
2. Over-deliver for client #1: check their ads daily, call them weekly, fix things fast.
3. Save proof: screenshots of leads, cost per lead, jobs booked. Use the **Case Study Template**.
4. Ask for a testimonial and referrals. Use the **Referral Texts**; the reward is a free month per signed referral.

## Phase 2: Make the offer easier to say yes to
5. **Stay in one niche and one region:** New England landscaping and tree companies.
6. **Add speed-to-lead:** an instant auto-text to each lead ("Thanks! [Owner] will call you shortly"). It roughly doubles how many leads turn into jobs.
7. **Raise prices after you have proof:** new clients at $800–1,000/month; keep the early clients at $600.
8. **Winter push:** pitch landscapers on spring pre-booking and add roofers and snow removal so you earn year-round.

## Phase 3: Stop doing everything yourself (5+ clients)
9. **Templates for everything:** onboarding, ads, weekly reports and agreements. New client setup should take about 2 hours.
10. **Hire a caller** (part-time VA, $5–8/hr, or pay per booked call). You close, they dial.
11. **Run ApexLeads' own ads** (see the ApexLeads Own Ads doc) so owners come to you.

## Avoid
- Mass cold emails: about 740 sent and zero positive replies, and they risk your Gmail account.
- Chasing every industry or state before you have 10 clients.
- Promising a number of leads. Promise the work, not the result.

## Your weekly rhythm
- **Mon:** send every client their weekly report.
- **Tue–Thu, mornings and evenings:** call 20–30 companies from the current call sheet.
- **Fri:** tell Claude who you called; update the pipeline; get new ad drafts for anyone interested.
- **Monthly:** case study plus referral ask for any client with a good month.
