Questions

Straight answers.

How much should I spend on ads?

Most landscapers start at $10–20 a day. We'll recommend a number based on your towns and services, and you can change it anytime.

Do you guarantee a number of leads?

No, and be careful with anyone who does. We promise the work: good ads, fast changes and an honest weekly report. The pay-per-lead option means you only pay when leads come in.

Do I need a Facebook page?

Yes, a business page. If yours needs work, we'll help you clean it up before ads go live.

What happens in winter?

We switch to spring pre-booking (patios, walls, spring cleanups) and snow removal, so you go into April with a full calendar.

Can I cancel?

Yes. It's month to month. Your page, ad account and leads stay yours.
