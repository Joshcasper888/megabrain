## 1. Prospects

**Who to target:** businesses already paying for Meta ads whose ads look self-run:
a generic headline, the business name as the headline, a single ad, no clear offer.
They already believe ads work and have budget, but they probably have nobody
optimizing them. Businesses with many polished variants of the same ad likely
already have an agency, so they're lower priority.

### Timing (late September)

- **Roofing:** fall is peak season. Homeowners want the roof done before winter, and
  storm and insurance-claim season is active. Pitch now.
- **Landscaping:** in northern states, lead with fall cleanup, leaf removal and
  booking spring hardscaping early. In TX, FL, AZ and SoCal it's year-round, so
  lead with turf, irrigation and hardscape.

## 2. Competition (other agencies advertising to contractors)

What they promise in their current US ads:

- "8–12 New Jobs Per Month — Guaranteed" (Wavelength Digital)
- "10-20 New Retaining Wall Projects Every Month" (Primara 365)
- "You Book Jobs - Or You Don't Pay"
- "$0 Upfront. Pay Per Show"
- "$100/Week Ads — Cancel Anytime" (Dirt Cheap Agency)
- "Signed contracts, not lead counts" (Kaizen Studio)

**What this means for you:** contractors hear "guaranteed jobs" every day and don't
trust it. As a new agency with no case studies, don't promise numbers you can't back
up. Compete on things you can actually deliver:

1. **Niche focus:** only roofers and landscapers.
2. **No long contract:** month to month.
3. **They own everything:** the ad account, Page and leads stay theirs, and you get
   partner access.
4. **Speed to lead:** set up instant alerts so they call leads back within 5 minutes.
   Contractors lose more jobs to slow follow-up than to bad ads.
5. **Plain weekly report:** leads, cost per lead, and booked estimates.

## 3. The offer

**Meta Ads Management for Roofers & Landscapers**

What's included:
- Full campaign setup in the client's own ad account: lead form or quote-request ads
- 3–5 ad variations per month, written and tested (copy, plus creative from their job photos)
- Instant lead alerts by text or email
- Weekly optimization, plus a one-page weekly report
- A monthly 20-minute call

What the client provides:
- Admin access to their Facebook Page, and partner access to their ad account
- Job photos and videos (before/after works best)
- The ad budget, paid by them directly to Meta

**Suggested pricing to start (adjust to your market):**

In exchange for the founding-client rate, ask for a testimonial and permission to
share their results. That gives you the case studies you don't have yet.

## 4. Outreach scripts

### Cold DM / email (prospect already running ads)

> Hi {first name}, I came across {business}'s ad on Facebook ("{their headline}").
> Good to see you're already running ads. Most contractors aren't.
>
> I only run Facebook and Instagram ads for roofers and landscapers. Looking at
> your ad, I'd test {one specific idea: a before/after photo, a clear offer like
> "free inspection + photo report", a lead form instead of sending people to your
> website}.
>
> Want me to put together 2–3 free ad ideas for {business}? No strings attached.
>
> {your name}

### Cold DM / email (prospect not running ads)

> Hi {first name}, I saw {business} has {X} great Google reviews but isn't running
> any Facebook ads. Your competitors {competitor name if you know one} are.
>
> I run Facebook ads only for roofers and landscapers, month to month, and you keep
> full ownership of the ad account. Open to a 15-minute call to see if it would work
> for you?

### Follow-up (3–4 days later)

> Hi {first name}, just following up. I mocked up an ad idea for {business} using
> {offer}. Want me to send it over?

**Tip:** actually send the mock-up. A concrete ad made for their business converts
far better than a generic pitch. I can draft these for each prospect.
